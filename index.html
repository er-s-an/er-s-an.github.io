04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Half systems.
Half instinct.

Caesar / Jiachen Xie / 谢佳辰

I care how things work.
And how they feel to use.

Most people call me Caesar. I’m a CityUHK student entrepreneur and CTO of MAKEBOOK, working across AI agents, creative tools, and games. I like following an idea through the whole build: what it does, how it works, and how it feels in someone’s hands.

The cut of a jacket, the spacing in a headline, the feel of a small interaction: I pay attention to all three. That attention follows me into code. I want the work to have character, and the buttons to do their jobs.

AI research & engineering internships

I’d like to intern with the Kimi team at Moonshot AI or another leading AI research lab. I’m especially interested in model post-training, agent evaluation, and training environments.

Research interests and selected work

I’m looking for Research Engineer Intern or AI Agent Engineering Intern roles. I’d like to build executable tasks and evaluation criteria for coding agents, particularly for web and game development. I’m open to any location and can adjust my studies for the right internship.

LLM agent evaluation and computer use

In my Kimi K3 evaluation, I used game prototypes and video editing tasks to examine task completion, visual feedback, and token cost. My Hunyuan HY4 evaluation tested long-horizon computer use and whether a model could turn a reference image into a playable web game.

Agent harnesses and tool calling

At Tencent Games LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS, I built an in-game ReAct agent layer with MCP-compatible actions. I also independently prototyped a Godot editor plugin that connects generative model APIs to the game asset workflow.

Web and game environments

For Kanshan Anywhere Door, our team built a story-to-game compiler with structured validation and an interactive runtime with deterministic game rules. The project reached the national finals of Zhihu Hackathon S2.

Open-source multimodal RAG

My RAG-Anything and LightRAG contributions reduced model calls during document ingestion and added discovery of installed custom chunking plugins.
